01 / ABOUT US

Crafting Wood.
Creating Timeless Spaces.

New Jangid Wood Works is a wood manufacturing and trading company based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. We specialize in teak wood doors, wooden windows, door frames, CNC wood carving and interior furniture.

We combine carefully selected wood, skilled craftsmanship and modern production techniques to deliver products for homes, commercial spaces and project requirements.

We serve homeowners, builders, architects, contractors, dealers and furniture professionals, with supply available across India.
